About Me

I am a M.S. student in Artificial Intelligence at Peking University (Sept. 2024 – present, expected June 2027). Before that, I received my B.S. in Computer Science and Technology from Jilin University. I conducted research at the PKU-Agibot Lab, advised by Prof. Hao Dong and Dr. Ruihai Wu.

My research centers on generalizable robotic manipulation, with a current focus on world action models (WAMs) learned from large-scale human and robot data. My broader interests include vision-language-action (VLA) models, visual affordance, and multi-modal policy learning.

I am currently building OpenWAM — a modular library for world-action model training, inference and deployment, covering multiple architecture families and benchmark evaluation. Open-source release is in preparation. Stay tuned!
